## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Harbor](https://goharbor.io/)**

  **Open-source container registry with governance and policy enforcement** (CNCF Graduated). **The most critical supply chain component for secure defense logistics**—Hardened Containers and Continuous Supply Chain (CSC) are central to logistics operations. Features vulnerability scanning (Trivy, Clair), image signing (Notary, Cosign), RBAC, audit logging, and artifact replication across air-gapped environments. **The foundation for secure software supply chains in defense contexts.**



- **[Sigstore](https://github.com/sigstore)**

  **Open-source software signing and verification infrastructure** (Apache-2.0, OpenSSF). **The most comprehensive supply chain integrity layer available.** Features **Cosign** for container and artifact signing, **Fulcio** for certificate authority, **Rekor** for immutable transparency log, and **Gitsign** for git commit signing. **Critical for defense logistics** where software provenance must be cryptographically verifiable across distributed supply chains.



- **[Kubernetes](https://kubernetes.io/)**

  **Open-source container orchestration platform** (Apache-2.0, CNCF Graduated). **The foundation for deploying and managing defense logistics workloads at scale.** Features self-healing, horizontal scaling, service discovery, and declarative configuration. **Runs on-prem, in the cloud, in air-gapped environments, or in the field**—essential for contested logistics where infrastructure is constrained.



- **[TensorFlow](https://www.tensorflow.org/)**

  **End-to-end open-source ML platform** (Apache-2.0). Used extensively for predictive maintenance, route optimization, and demand forecasting in logistics. Supports **TensorFlow Lite** for edge deployment on autonomous platforms and **TensorFlow.js** for browser-based inference.



- **[PyTorch](https://pytorch.org/)**

  **Leading open-source deep learning framework** (BSD license). **The dominant framework for autonomous systems research and production**—used by defense organizations worldwide for computer vision, sensor fusion, and reinforcement learning.



- **[Ray](https://www.ray.io/)**

  **Distributed computing framework for AI workloads** (Apache-2.0). **The most widely used orchestration layer for distributed training and inference in defense applications.** Features Ray Train for distributed training, Ray Serve for model serving, and Ray Tune for hyperparameter optimization. **Scalable from a single node to thousands.**



- **[OpenVINO](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/developer/tools/openvino-toolkit/overview.html)**

  **Intel's toolkit for optimizing and deploying AI inference** (Apache-2.0). **Critical for edge deployment on autonomous platforms** where power, compute, and thermal constraints are tight. Features model optimization, quantization, and hardware-specific acceleration.



- **[Apache Kafka](https://kafka.apache.org/)**

  **Distributed event streaming platform** (Apache-2.0). **The backbone for real-time logistics event streaming**—supply chain telemetry, sensor data, location updates, and status changes. Features high-throughput, fault-tolerant, and persistent messaging. **Used by defense organizations for secure data pipelines.**



- **[Apache NiFi](https://nifi.apache.org/)**

  **Data flow automation and orchestration platform** (Apache-2.0). **The most powerful open-source tool for automating data movement between disparate logistics systems.** Features visual flow design, data provenance, and prioritization. **Used across government and defense for secure data integration.**



- **[PostGIS](https://postgis.net/)**

  **Spatial database extension for PostgreSQL** (GPL-2.0). **The foundation for geospatial logistics operations**—route planning, asset tracking, territory analysis, and map-based visualization. **Used by government and defense organizations worldwide.**



- **[GRASS GIS](https://grass.osgeo.org/)**

  **Open-source Geographic Information System** (GPL-2.0). **The most mature open-source GIS for terrain analysis, viewshed calculation, and route planning.** Features raster/vector analysis, 3D visualization, and time-series processing. **Used by militaries and government agencies globally.**



- **[QGIS](https://qgis.org/)**

  **Open-source GIS platform** (GPL-2.0). **The most user-friendly open-source GIS** with extensive plugin ecosystem. Features map creation, spatial analysis, and data visualization. **Widely used for logistics planning and situational awareness.**



- **[OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/)**

  **Open-source map of the world** (ODbL). **The foundational geospatial dataset for logistics routing** where commercial map data may be unavailable or restricted. Features global coverage, community-maintained, and free to use with attribution.



- **[Project OSRM](https://project-osrm.org/)**

  **Open-source routing engine** (BSD-2-Clause). **The most widely deployed open-source routing engine**—used by logistics companies and government agencies. Features fast routing, isochrone generation, and turn-by-turn directions. **Runs entirely offline** with OpenStreetMap data.



- **[Valhalla](https://github.com/valhalla/valhalla)**

  **Open-source routing engine** (MIT) with multi-modal support (auto, bicycle, pedestrian, transit). Features isochrones, time-distance matrices, and map matching. **The most flexible open-source routing engine for complex logistics scenarios.**



- **[Apache Airflow](https://airflow.apache.org/)**

  **Workflow orchestration platform** (Apache-2.0). **The most widely used open-source workflow scheduler**—used for logistics pipeline orchestration, ETL, and ML training pipelines. Features DAG-based scheduling, backfilling, and rich UI.



- **[Prefect](https://www.prefect.io/)**

  **Modern workflow orchestration platform** (Apache-2.0). **The most developer-friendly open-source workflow engine** for dynamic, data-intensive logistics workflows. Features Python-native DAGs, async execution, and hybrid cloud orchestration.



- **[Dagster](https://dagster.io/)**

  **Data orchestration platform** (Apache-2.0). **The most asset-aware open-source orchestrator**—treats data assets as first-class citizens with lineage, observability, and testing. **Ideal for logistics data pipelines requiring traceability.**



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **[Apache Arrow](https://arrow.apache.org/)** — Cross-language development platform for in-memory data (Apache-2.0). **The standard for high-performance data exchange** in logistics analytics pipelines.

- **[Apache Parquet](https://parquet.apache.org/)** — Columnar storage format (Apache-2.0). **The standard for efficient logistics data storage and analytics.**

- **[Delta Lake](https://delta.io/)** — Open-source storage framework (Apache-2.0). **ACID transactions and versioning for data lakes**—critical for audit trails in defense logistics.

- **[Apache Iceberg](https://iceberg.apache.org/)** — Open table format (Apache-2.0). **The emerging standard for large-scale analytics tables**—enables multiple engines to access the same data.

- **[Open Policy Agent (OPA)](https://www.openpolicyagent.org/)** — Policy engine (Apache-2.0, CNCF Graduated). **The standard for policy-as-code enforcement**—critical for access control and compliance in defense logistics.

- **[Vault](https://www.vaultproject.io/)** — Secrets management platform (BUSL 1.1). **The standard for secrets management**—critical for securing credentials across distributed logistics systems.

- **[Keycloak](https://www.keycloak.org/)** — Identity and access management (Apache-2.0). **The most widely used open-source IAM**—critical for SSO and access control in defense systems.

- **[SPIFFE/SPIRE](https://spiffe.io/)** — Workload identity (Apache-2.0, CNCF Graduated). **The standard for zero-trust workload identity**—critical for service-to-service authentication in distributed logistics.

- Community **n8n** workflows for logistics event routing and alert automation.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **Kubernetes** for orchestration with **Harbor** and **Sigstore** for secure supply chain, **Apache Kafka** for event streaming, **PostGIS + Project OSRM** for geospatial routing, **Ray** for distributed ML workloads, and **Open Policy Agent** for policy enforcement—creating a fully self-hosted, enterprise-grade autonomous defense logistics platform.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Defense logistics systems handling classified or controlled unclassified information (CUI) must comply with **ITAR, EAR, NIST 800-171, CMMC, and applicable security controls**. **Agency authorization and accreditation remain mandatory** before deployment.

- **Open-source defense logistics is foundational infrastructure, not end-to-end platforms.** Harbor and Sigstore provide secure supply chain . Kubernetes provides orchestration . Kafka and NiFi provide data streaming and flow . PostGIS, OSRM, and Valhalla provide geospatial routing . **No single open-source tool provides the integrated command-and-control of Anduril Lattice or Palantir Gotham.** Integration work is required.

- **Supply chain security is non-negotiable.** Sigstore's cryptographic signing and immutable transparency log provide verifiable provenance from build to deployment . Harbor's vulnerability scanning and image signing enforce policy at the registry . **CMMC compliance requires demonstrable software supply chain integrity.**

- **Contested environments demand offline-first capabilities.** OSRM runs entirely offline with OpenStreetMap data . TensorFlow Lite and OpenVINO deploy to edge platforms with constrained resources . Kubernetes supports air-gapped clusters . **Design for degraded communications from the start.**

- **Geospatial routing is the open-source sweet spot.** Project OSRM and Valhalla are used by logistics companies and government agencies worldwide . OpenStreetMap provides global coverage where commercial map data may be restricted . **These are genuine alternatives to commercial routing engines.**

- Self-hosted open-source solutions require proper security hardening, SBOM generation, and ongoing maintenance. **Defense logistics infrastructure is a high-value target**—zero-trust architecture, workload identity (SPIFFE), and secrets management (Vault) are essential.
